## Highlights

- **Reader language override:** choose Auto, English, 日本語, Deutsch, Français, Español or 简体中文 per Jellyfin user.
- **Cleaner mobile reader:** reduce top-bar clutter and give book metadata more room.
- **Simpler settings:** Reader Settings are now grouped and collapsible.
- **Smoother metadata motion:** long titles and metadata no longer visibly bounce back and forth.

## Reader UI & UX

- Replace the previous ping-pong metadata animation with:
  - a short pause at the beginning;
  - one-direction scrolling;
  - a pause at the end; and
  - an invisible fade/reset back to the start.
- Use compact floating reader chrome on narrow screens.
- Hide duplicate page-position information from the mobile top bar.
- Keep the bottom scrubber as the primary page-position control.
- Move secondary **Fullscreen** and **Help** actions into a localized **…** menu on mobile.

## Reader Settings

Settings are organized into five sections:

- **Reading** — layout and reading direction.
- **Appearance** — fit, zoom, background, side padding and page gap.
- **Behavior** — transitions and touch gestures.
- **Metadata** — title/author/series/issue/year visibility and auto-scroll.
- **Language** — automatic detection or an explicit reader language.

On mobile, the sections behave as an accordion so only the active category remains expanded.
