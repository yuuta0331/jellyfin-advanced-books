## Highlights

- **Jellyfin-native book actions:** optionally open Advanced Reader from Jellyfin's normal Resume/Play and Start from beginning controls.
- **Natural zoom:** pinch, Ctrl+wheel and double tap now zoom around the point you are actually looking at.
- **Komga-style navigation:** paged layouts use left/right zones; Vertical/Webtoon use top/bottom zones.
- **Desktop grab interaction:** grab and drag continuous pages or scrollable paged canvases with the mouse.
- **Cleaner navigation chrome:** the bottom bar collapses to a compact page-position state when idle.

## Jellyfin Integration

- Browser Back and Android/browser back gestures close Advanced Reader and reveal the underlying Jellyfin screen.
- Reader Close/Escape consumes the synthetic reader-history entry instead of navigating away.
- Add the optional **Use Advanced Reader for Jellyfin book actions** setting.
- On supported CBZ/ZIP books:
  - Jellyfin Resume/Play opens Advanced Reader at saved progress.
  - Start from beginning opens page 1 without restoring saved progress.
- Unsupported books continue using Jellyfin's built-in reader.
- Replace baseline-sensitive previous/next glyphs with centered SVG chevrons.

## Navigation & Page Transitions

- Single / Double Page:
  - left/right click or tap zones navigate according to RTL/LTR reading direction;
  - the center zone toggles reader controls; and
  - page changes use a short directional horizontal transition when Page transitions is enabled.
- Vertical Continuous / Webtoon:
  - top/bottom click or tap zones move to the previous/next tracked page;
  - navigation uses smooth scrolling; and
  - the center zone toggles reader controls.
- Reduced-motion users receive immediate movement instead of animated transitions.

## Zoom & Pan

- Two-finger pinch keeps the pinch midpoint visually anchored.
- Ctrl+wheel zoom keeps the mouse pointer location as the focal point.
- Double tap zooms the tapped area to 200%.
- A second double tap restores the previous/base zoom.
- One-finger touch pan works after zoom in paged and continuous layouts.
- Vertical/Webtoon can be grabbed and dragged with the mouse at any zoom.
- Paged Fit Width / Original can use mouse grab-scroll whenever the canvas overflows.
- Existing >100% paged pan remains available.

## Reader UI & Controls

- Bottom navigation now has two states:
  - active: Previous / scrubber / page position / Next;
  - idle: compact page position only, with the thin progress rail still visible.
- Mobile Fullscreen and Help remain under the compact **…** menu.
- Help text now documents mode-aware tap zones, mouse drag and double-tap zoom.
- Help/localization updates cover all six supported languages.

## Reliability & Validation

- Keep host/history integration isolated in the dedicated Jellyfin Integration bridge.
- Keep zoom/touch/mouse interaction work isolated in the Gestures bridge so the size-constrained Core reader does not grow.
- Prevent drag completion from firing a navigation tap through a synthetic click.
- Prevent pointer cancellation and swipe movement from being interpreted as taps.
- Extend localization/integration source guards and manual smoke-test coverage.

## Updating

- Existing reader preferences and progress remain compatible.
- The native Jellyfin book-action replacement remains **off by default**.
- Restart Jellyfin after updating the plugin.
